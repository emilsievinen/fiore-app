Fiore privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Fiore is a free, educational, not-for-profit iPhone app with reference content for the art of Fiore dei Liberi and a private training log. It is made by Emil Sievinen, an individual developer.

The short version

Fiore collects no data. Nothing you do or write in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else. If you send us feedback, see Feedback you send us.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your training log. The sessions you record (date, duration, kind, effort, location, notes and what you practised) are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. The developer cannot see them.
  • What you study. The entries you star for Working on, your notes on plays, your recall practice cards, the Start here steps you tick and your lesson plans are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Your settings. Choices such as the app's appearance are stored only on your iPhone.

If you have iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup turned on for your iPhone, Apple's backup may include the app's data, under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.

Voice logging

If you log a session by voice in Quick log, the app asks for the microphone and speech recognition. Your speech is turned into text on your iPhone. The audio is not kept and is not sent to the developer, to Apple or to anyone else. You can refuse and type instead.

The first time you use voice, iOS may download Apple's speech recognition model for English. That download comes from Apple to your iPhone and carries nothing you say or write.

Quick log may use Apple's on-device language model to work out what your sentence means. It runs on your iPhone, and your sentence is not sent anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No third-party software development kits.
  • Nothing is sent over the network. The app works offline.
  • No other permissions. The app does not ask for location, contacts, photos or notifications.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's settings (Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and anonymous usage and performance statistics for this app with the developer. Apple collects and anonymises them under its own privacy policy, they do not include your training log, notes or anything you write, and you can turn sharing off at any time. The app itself sends nothing.

If you test the app through TestFlight, Apple also sends the developer the crash reports, screenshots and feedback you choose to send from TestFlight.

Feedback you send us

If you send feedback or a crash report through TestFlight, write an App Store review, or email fiore-app@pm.me, the developer reads it to fix problems and decide what to improve. Notes kept for that purpose are summaries without your name or email address.

TestFlight feedback stays in Apple's App Store Connect, and emails stay in the developer's mailbox, only as long as they are needed for that purpose. The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in improving the app you chose to test or review.

You can ask what is held about you, or ask for it to be deleted, at fiore-app@pm.me, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in Finland, Tietosuojavaltuutettu, the Data Protection Ombudsman).

Export and import

You can export your training log as a file and choose where it goes with the iOS share sheet, and import such a file with the iOS file picker. The file goes only where you send it. The app never sends it anywhere by itself.

Lesson plans and class handouts are shared the same way, only when you choose to share them. A lesson plan file someone sends you is read only when you open it in Fiore.

Links

The Credits and licences screen has links to the manuscript's page at the J. Paul Getty Museum and to licence pages. They open in your web browser, outside the app, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

You can delete any session in the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to fiore-app@pm.me or through the support link on the app's App Store page.
