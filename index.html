Fiore

A pocket book, practice partner and training log for the art of Fiore dei Liberi, the Italian master whose Fior di Battaglia was written around 1410. For people who study Historical European Martial Arts (HEMA) and the instructors who teach them.

The app follows the Getty manuscript (MS Ludwig XV 13) with Fiore's Italian terms first and plain English beside them. It has his guards, blows and plays with their figures, recall practice, a drill caller, lesson plans for instructors and a private training log. It is free, educational and not for profit, for iPhone.

Support and contact

Questions, problems or ideas: write to fiore-app@pm.me. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use if something does not work.

Privacy

Fiore collects no data. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing you write leaves your iPhone unless you share it. Read the privacy policy.

Sources

The figures and pages are the J. Paul Getty Museum's images of the manuscript, released as public domain (CC0 1.0) under its Open Content Program. The explanations are original summaries written for the app, not translations. Every source is listed in the app under Credits and licences.
